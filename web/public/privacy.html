Criminals HQ: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Criminals HQ (criminals-hq.vercel.app) is a private planning dashboard for the Criminals FIRST LEGO League robotics team (2026–27 season). Team members use it to manage tasks, milestones, risks, weekly reviews and documentation. It is not a public service and only invited team members can sign in.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive only the basic profile information Google shares with the openid, email and profile permissions:

  • your email address,
  • your name,
  • your Google profile picture, used as your default profile photo.

You can replace that photo with one you upload yourself, or remove it. Your profile photo is saved as a small image in the team's sheet and is visible only to signed-in team members. Only you can change your own profile.

We do not receive your Google password and we do not get access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

While you use the dashboard, we also store the content you create: task and planning edits, your name next to each change (activity log), and any files you choose to upload.

2. How we use it

  • Your email is checked against the team's member list to decide whether you can sign in.
  • Your name is shown next to the changes and uploads you make, so the team knows who did what.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone outside the team.

3. Where it is stored

All data is stored in the team's own Google Sheet and Google Drive folder, which are managed by the team. The website is hosted on Vercel and runs on Google Apps Script. These providers process data only to run the service.

4. Google user data

Criminals HQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google profile data is used only to sign you in and show your name inside the dashboard.

5. How long we keep it

Data is kept for the current season. You can ask us at any time to remove your account, your name or your uploads, and we will delete them.

6. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: monzerabdelgawad@gmail.com
